# A4. Definition of the Analytic Population

## Eligibility criteria

The primary analytic population consists of NHANES participants who meet all of the following criteria:

1. Have records in `DEMO_L`, `SLQ_L`, and `DSQTOT_L`, linked by `SEQN`.
2. Are aged 20 years or older, based on `RIDAGEYR` in `DEMO_L`.
3. Have a positive `WTDRD1` dietary sample weight.
4. Have a valid yes/no response to `DSD010` in `DSQTOT_L`.
5. Have a usable value for the sleep outcome being analyzed.

For the sleep-duration means, only values from 3 to 13.5 hours are treated as exact numeric values. NHANES uses `2` to represent less than 3 hours and `14` to represent 14 hours or more. These grouped categories, and missing sleep values, are excluded from analyses of the numeric mean.

The number of participants with a valid sleep-duration value depends on the outcome:

| Sleep outcome | Participants with usable sleep data | Supplement use reported (`DSD010 = 1`) | No supplement use reported (`DSD010 = 2`) | Excluded for missing or grouped sleep value |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Weekday/workday sleep (`SLD012`) | 4,699 | 3,051 | 1,648 | 76 |
| Weekend/non-workday sleep (`SLD013`) | 4,690 | 3,047 | 1,643 | 85 |

Among the 76 participants excluded from the weekday/workday numeric-mean analysis, 38 had a missing sleep value, 23 had the grouped value for less than 3 hours, and 15 had the grouped value for 14 hours or more. Among the 85 participants excluded from the weekend/non-workday numeric-mean analysis, 42 had a missing value, 20 had the grouped value for less than 3 hours, and 23 had the grouped value for 14 hours or more.

A total of 4,666 participants have usable numeric values for both sleep outcomes. The weekday and weekend outcomes will otherwise be analyzed using their outcome-specific sample sizes shown above.

## Handling of exclusions

Participants younger than 20 years are excluded because the project’s target population is adults. Participants with a zero `WTDRD1` are excluded from the weighted analytic population; NHANES identifies a zero dietary day one weight with a Day 1 dietary recall that was not completed. Participants with refused, “don't know,” or missing responses to `DSD010` are excluded because they cannot be assigned to the supplement-use or no-supplement-use group. Participants with missing or grouped sleep-duration values are excluded only from analyses of that numeric sleep outcome.

These counts describe the current analytic rules applied to the downloaded files. Any secondary analysis, such as an ingredient-specific melatonin analysis, will require its own eligibility counts and may have a smaller analytic sample.

Sources: [NHANES `DSQTOT_L` documentation](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/Nchs/Data/Nhanes/Public/2021/DataFiles/DSQTOT_L.htm) explains the use of `WTDRD1` for 30-day supplement analyses and identifies zero weights with incomplete or uncompleted Day 1 dietary recalls. [NHANES `SLQ_L` documentation](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/Nchs/Data/Nhanes/Public/2021/DataFiles/SLQ_L.htm) documents the sleep-duration variables and their grouped values for less than 3 hours and 14 hours or more.
